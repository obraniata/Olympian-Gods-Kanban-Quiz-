How to play

Five levels, 12 points each: 60 points in all.

  1. Pick a tile. On a computer, drag a tile onto a god card. On a phone or tablet, tap the tile first, then tap the card.
  2. Level 1 is faces. Match each name to the picture. From Level 2 on, every card shows its god, and you match the domain, the symbol, the Roman name and the story clue.
  3. Check your answers. Green is correct. Red is wrong: tap a red card to take the tile back, then try again.
  4. Submit each level to unlock the next one. Your scores are saved for your teacher.
